# Balance history

Facts only. Dates and what changed — don't worry about writing it nicely,
that gets turned into readable copy on the card page.

## Why this file exists

Balance changes are **facts**: a date and a number that moved. Facts can't be
copyrighted, so gathering them from anywhere is fine. What *is* protected is
someone else's written commentary about them, so please don't paste prose —
just the bare change.

**Good sources**

- Supercell's official balance-change posts. Primary source, most reliable.
- RoyaleAPI's balance history pages — they block bots, not people, so read
  them in your browser.

**Avoid the Fandom wiki.** Its text is CC BY-SA, which is the exact licensing
problem we removed from the card hints. Facts read from it are fine; copied
sentences are not.

## Format

One `##` heading per card, matching the card's name in `cards.json` exactly.
Then one bullet per change:

    - YYYY-MM-DD — what changed

Anything after the date is free text. Prefixing with Released / Buff / Nerf /
Rework helps but isn't required. A trailing `(source: ...)` is welcome — it
lets me flag anything that looks off rather than taking it on trust.

Lines that don't start with `-` are ignored, so notes to yourself are safe.

Only fill in cards that are getting a page. There's no value in doing all 121.

# Arrows

## Balance history
- 2016-02-02 — Damage reduced by 4%.
- 2016-02-19 — Crown Tower damage reduced from 50% to 40% of full damage.
- 2017-03-13 — Projectile speed increased from 600 to 800.
- 2017-12-12 — Crown Tower damage reduced from 40% to 35% of full damage.
- 2019-06-03 — Projectile speed increased from 800 to 1100.
- 2019-11-26 — Damage per volley reduced, changed from 1 hit to 3 volleys, total damage increased by 23%.
- 2020-08-04 — Crown Tower damage reduced from 35% to 30% of full damage.
- 2022-10-26 — Damage area behavior changed to multiple smaller areas; effective radius unchanged.
- 2024-08-06 — Crown Tower damage reduced from 30% to 25% of full damage.
- 2025-03-04 — Radius reduced from 4 to 3.5 tiles.

## Counters
- No direct counter once cast.
- Best defended by not giving spell value.
- Spread swarm placements.
- Stagger support troops.
- Higher-health troops that survive the volleys.

## Synergies
- Graveyard
- Goblin Barrel
- Skeleton Barrel
- Prince
- Dark Prince
- P.E.K.K.A.
- Mini P.E.K.K.A.
- Golden Knight
- Firecracker
- Magic Archer

## Strategy notes
- Best used to clear swarm defenders and fragile support.
- Strong answer to Goblin Barrel, Minion Horde, Skeleton Army, Bats, and Goblin Gang.
- Often used to open a path for single-target push cards.
- More reliable than many small spells because of large area coverage.
